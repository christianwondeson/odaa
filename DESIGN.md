# DESIGN
## Audience and intent (written BEFORE generating)
Audience: women and girls of my generation (and their families) watching on a phone, vertical, via WhatsApp/TikTok.
Intent: they should feel seen, and leave believing a child wounded by absence, favoritism, and harsh labor can still rise into dignity—without erasing herself to earn love.
Logline: An Ethiopian girl—wounded by an absent father, separated from her mother, and pushed to exhaustion in an unjust remarried household—discovers no one is coming to save her, yet rises as a brilliant student, communicator, and designer.
Why fiction: inspired by a real life; identifying details omitted and no real person's face or voice is depicted without recorded consent (see ETHICS.md).

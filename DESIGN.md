# DESIGN
## Audience and intent (written BEFORE generating, narrowed after Tester 2 feedback)
Audience: young Ethiopian women (ages 16–28) raised in blended or step-family households who carry silent duty and people-pleasing wounds, watching vertical 9:16 video on WhatsApp/TikTok. *(Narrowed from "women and girls of my generation and their families" after Tester 2 noted the target audience was too broad for the story to hit deeply.)*
Intent: they should feel seen, recognize that surviving without someone coming to rescue you does not mean disappearing for others, and leave knowing they can grow from a solitary seed into the Odaa shelter they never had.
Logline: An Ethiopian girl—wounded by an absent father, separated from her mother, and pushed to exhaustion in an unjust remarried household—discovers no one is coming to save her, yet rises as a brilliant student, communicator, and designer.
Why fiction: inspired by a real life; identifying details omitted and no real person's face or voice is depicted without recorded consent (see ETHICS.md).

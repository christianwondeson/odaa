# DESIGN
## Audience and intent (written BEFORE generating)
Audience: women and girls of my generation (and their families) watching on a phone, vertical, via WhatsApp/TikTok.
Intent: they should feel seen, and leave believing a child nobody protected can still learn that her worth is not what she gives.
Logline: A girl overlooked in her own home discovers her worth was never measured by how much she gives.
Why fiction: inspired by a real life; no real person's face or voice is shown or cloned (see ETHICS.md).

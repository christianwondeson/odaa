# Amharic Narration Script (for Review & Optional Voice Recording)

Title: **Under the Odaa / በኦዳ ዛፍ ሥር**

Read each shot aloud naturally at an unhurried, intimate pace.
- If you want to change any Amharic line, edit it in `storyboard.json` first.
- If you record your own voice reading these lines, save each clip as:
  `outputs/voice_override/shot_01.wav` (or `.mp3` / `.m4a`) through `shot_10` — the assembly stage will automatically use your recording when present while keeping AI TTS (`pipeline.narration`) reproducible for anyone who clones the repo.
- Remember to record your voice consent in `ETHICS.md` if you include your real voice in the final video.

---

### Shot 01 (~6s) — The Seed Separated from Shade
- **Amharic:** ከኦዳ ዛፍ ጥላ ተለይቶ፣ አንድ ዘር በደረቅ መሬት ውስጥ ብቻውን ወደቀ።
- **English:** Separated from the Odaa's shade, a single seed fell alone into dry earth.

### Shot 02 (~8s) — Absent Father, Separation from Mother & People-Pleasing
- **Amharic:** ገና በልጅነቷ የአባት እቅፍ ጎደላት፤ ከእናቷም መለየቷ ልቧን አደማው። ሁሉንም ለማስደሰት ራሷን መርሳት ጀመረች።
- **English:** Growing up without a father beside her—and separated from her mother—she learned to shrink herself just to please everyone around her.

### Shot 03 (~8s) — Mother's Remarriage & Unfair Punishment
- **Amharic:** እናቷ ሌላ ትዳር መሥርታ ከሷ ጋር እንድትኖር ስትፈቅድላት ተስፋ አደረገች። ግን የቀድሞውን ትዳር በሷ እያየች፣ ያለምንም ጥፋት ትቀጣት ነበር።
- **English:** When her mother remarried and allowed her to live with her, she hoped for warmth. Instead, seeing the past in her firstborn, her mother punished her for no fault.

### Shot 04 (~9s) — Favoritism Toward the 3 Half-Siblings
- **Amharic:** አዲሱ ትዳር ሦስት ልጆች ሲያፈራ፣ ስጦታውና ፍቅሩ ለነሱ ብቻ ሆነ። እሷ ግን በራሷ ቤት ውስጥ የማትፈለግ እንግዳ ሆነች።
- **English:** When three younger children were born, every gift and tender glance went to them—leaving her unseen in her own home.

### Shot 05 (~8s) — Worked to Exhaustion Caring for the Siblings
- **Amharic:** የታናናሽ ልጆቹን ልብስ በማጠብና ቤቱን በማገልገል እስከ ሞት ደረሰች። ልጅነቷ በከባድ ሥራና በእንባ አለቀ።
- **English:** Washing her half-siblings' clothes and tending the house to the point of collapse, her childhood slipped away in labor and tears.

### Shot 06 (~7s) — "No One Is Coming to Save Me"
- **Amharic:** በዛ መከራ ውስጥ ዓለም ጨካኝ እንደሆነች ተረዳች፤ ማንም መጥቶ እንደማያድናትም አወቀች።
- **English:** In those life-crushing days, she realized how cruel the world could be—and that no one was coming to save her.

### Shot 07 (~9s) — Full of Life: Self-Rescue, Student, Speaker & Designer
- **Amharic:** ስለዚህ ራሷን ለማዳን ወሰነች። ሕመሟን ወደ ትጋት ቀይራ፣ ጎበዝ ተማሪ፣ ደፋር ተናጋሪና ፈጣን ዲዛይነር ሆና ወጣች።
- **English:** So she chose to save herself. Turning pain into quiet grit, she excelled at school, spoke boldly, and became a swift designer.

### Shot 08 (~5s) — Hidden Roots Drinking Deep
- **Amharic:** በጭቆና ውስጥ ተደብቆ የነበረው ሥሯ ውኃ አግኝቶ በጥልቅ አደገ።
- **English:** Beneath the hardest stone, her hidden roots drank deep and held fast.

### Shot 09 (~8s) — Leading Her Own Design Craft
- **Amharic:** ዛሬ የራሷን የዲዛይን ሥራ የምትመራ፣ በግልጽ የምትናገርና ክብሯን የጠበቀች ጠንካራ ሴት ሆናለች።
- **English:** Today she leads her own design craft—speaking with clarity, standing in dignity, no longer erasing herself to be loved.

### Shot 10 (~9s) — Seed to Odaa Shelter (Clear Closing Message)
- **Amharic:** ያችን ትንሽ ዘር አስታውሱ—ራስን ማሳነስ አቁማ፣ ለብዙዎች የሚተርፍ የኦዳ ጥላ ሆነች። ዋጋሽ ለሌሎች በምትሰጪው አይለካም።
- **English:** Remember that seed—she stopped shrinking to earn love, and grew into the Odaa shelter for many. Your worth is never measured by what you give.
